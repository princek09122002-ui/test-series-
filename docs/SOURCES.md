# Official references and syllabus assumptions

Reference check: **3 November 2026**, India time.

- [Official NTA NEET portal](https://neet.nta.nic.in/).
- [NEET UG 2026 Information Bulletin, linked from the official portal](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s37bc1ec1d9c3426357e69acd5bf320061/uploads/2026/02/202602231394640855.pdf).

The bulletin’s examination scheme (printed pages 21–22; PDF pages 26–27 by one-based page count) specifies 180 compulsory questions in 180 minutes, with Physics 45, Chemistry 45, and Biology 90; +4 correct, −1 incorrect, and 0 unanswered. The front matter also presents this distribution and a maximum of 720. It describes pen-and-paper examination delivery. Online tests here are independent simulations.

Appendix III contains the official subject syllabus. The seeded taxonomy is deliberately **illustrative**, not a copied claim of complete official coverage. Class and NCERT chapter labels are a product mapping for navigation, not a claim that every coaching-material label matches an official syllabus boundary.

Before production use, an academic content owner should import and review the complete applicable syllabus, map every question’s concepts and prerequisites, verify original/licensed rights, and publish a new syllabus version. Future exam-year rules must be checked against the corresponding NTA bulletin and configured as a new blueprint rather than edited across UI files.
